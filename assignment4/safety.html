Safety and Identification

Safety rules

  1. Never eat a mushroom based on one feature alone.
  2. Look at the cap, underside, habitat, and season.
  3. If you are not sure, leave it alone.

Good beginner habits

  • Take pictures from more than one angle.
  • Write down where it was found.
  • Use multiple sources for comparison.

Observation categories

Category Example Why it helps
Cap surface Smooth or pitted Important for species comparison
Underside Gills, pores, or folds Closer observation is crucial for identifying edible mushrooms from their poisonous counterparts
Habitat Soil, stump, or log Habitat can help tell similiar mushrooms apart

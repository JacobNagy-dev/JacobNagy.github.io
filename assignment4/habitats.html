Habitats and Seasons

Spring woods

Damp soil and warming temperatures help early mushrooms appear in the spring.

Summer forests

Humidity and shade can support many mushrooms on logs, trunks, and woodland floors.

Fall leaf litter

Autumn is one of the busiest times for mushroom growth because of cool air and moisture.

Dead wood

Some species stay visible for long periods because they grow directly on decaying wood.

Habitat clues

  • Was it growing on soil, wood, or a living tree?
  • Was the area dry, wet, shaded, or open?
  • What month was it found?

Why clues matter

Two mushrooms can look similar, but their habitat and season may be very different. Recording those details helps narrow the possibilities.

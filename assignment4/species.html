Common Mushroom Species

Morel

Morels are known for their honeycomb-shaped caps and are most often seen in spring.

  • Season: Spring
  • Habitat: Woodland floor
  • Clue: Pitted cap

Chicken of the Woods

This mushroom is bright orange or yellow and usually grows in shelf-like clusters on wood.

  • Season: Summer to fall
  • Habitat: Tree trunks
  • Clue: Bright layered shelves

Turkey Tail

Turkey tail is a thin bracket fungus with bands of color and is often found on dead logs.

  • Season: Often year-round
  • Habitat: Dead wood
  • Clue: Fan shape with stripes
